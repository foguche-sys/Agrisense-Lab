How k-means works

  1. Put every feature on the same scale, so EC (hundreds) doesn't drown out moisture (tens).
  2. Pick {{ k }} starting points that are far apart.
  3. Give each tray to its nearest point. Move each point to the middle of its trays.
  4. Repeat until nothing moves. Each group is a zone.

Think about it

  1. Write one watering plan per zone instead of one per tray. What changes?
  2. Switch from 2 to 3 zones. Which tray moves, and why?
  3. Which feature seems to decide the zones most?
  4. A real farm does this with hundreds of sensors or soil maps. What would it save?
