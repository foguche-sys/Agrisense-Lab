Train it in Teachable Machine

  1. Collect. Photograph leaves from your trays or the school garden: healthy, yellowing (often low nitrogen) and pest damage. Aim for 30 or more of each, in different light.
  2. Keep some back. Put 10 photos of each kind aside. The model must never see them while it trains: they are your test set.
  3. Train. Open Teachable Machine → Image project in Chrome. Make three classes with the names below, upload the training photos and press Train model. It runs in the browser, so it works on Chromebooks.
  4. Test. Show it each test photo one at a time and write down what it guessed in the "Record your test" table.
  5. Improve. Look at the mistakes. Add more photos of the kind it gets wrong, retrain, test again and save the new result.

Photos stay in your browser with Teachable Machine; nothing is uploaded to AgriSense Lab. Only the counts in the table are saved here.

Think about it

  1. Why must the test photos be kept out of training?
  2. Your model was trained on your school's leaves. Would it work on a farm 500 miles away?
  3. Yellow leaves can mean low nitrogen, too much water or old age. Can a photo alone tell them apart? What sensor reading would help?
