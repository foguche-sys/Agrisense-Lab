The question

The AI Lab can already predict when a tray needs water, and the irrigation advisor suggests how many seconds to pump. Right now a teacher must approve every pump run{% if s.waiting %} ({{ s.waiting }} waiting now){% endif %}. Should the model be allowed to water on its own?

Take a side

  1. The model decides. It never forgets, works at night and learns from every reading.
  2. The model suggests, a person approves. How AgriSense Lab works today.
  3. The farmer decides. They know things the sensors can't: rain forecast, crop price, a broken pipe.

Use your evidence

  1. Who was more accurate in your class: the line or the students? By how much?
  2. When the line was wrong, what did it not know about (sun, heat, roots, a probe out of the soil)?
  3. What is the cost of a mistake in each direction: watering too early, or too late?
  4. Who is responsible if an automatic system floods a field or lets a crop wilt?
  5. What would have to be true before you let the model water on its own?
