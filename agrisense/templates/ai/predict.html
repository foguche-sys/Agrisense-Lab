How the line is found

The AI Lab draws the straight line that sits closest to all {{ p.n }} readings (the least-squares line). Its slope is how fast the tray dries.

{% if p.slope is defined %}

R² close to 1 means the readings sit almost on the line. Below about 0.7, trust the prediction less: the tray may be drying unevenly (sun, heat, roots).

Check it yourself: download the data, make a scatter chart in Google Sheets or Excel and add a linear trendline. You should get the same slope.
