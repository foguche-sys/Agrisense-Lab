Privacy at AgriSense Lab

What we keep about students. A first name with last initial, the class and team, a picture password (stored only as a one-way hash), lab journals, observations and the soil readings their team works with. No student email, birthday or address is needed.

Who sees it. The student's own team, their teachers, and their school's admins. Parents see only their own child. Researchers and observers see de-identified data only. AgriSense Hub support sees a school only when the school grants time-limited access for a ticket, and every view is logged.

Downloads. Exports are de-identified by default. Named exports are limited to teachers and school admins and are recorded in the school's activity log.

Retention. Each school sets how long readings are kept (365 days by default). A school can ask for all its data to be exported or deleted.

Agreements. We sign a student data privacy agreement with each district or school (in the School → Privacy page) before rostering students.
