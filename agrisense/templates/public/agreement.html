Student Data Privacy Agreement: key terms

We sign a privacy agreement with every district or school before students are added. We prefer your standard: send us your district's National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA v2.2) or your state alliance's version and we sign it. If you have none, we use our own agreement, with the terms below.

TermWhat we commit to
OwnershipStudent data belongs to the school. We are a FERPA school official under the school's direct control.
UseOnly to provide, secure and support AgriSense Lab. No selling, no advertising, no profiling, no AI training on student data.
ParentsWe help the school answer a parent's request to see or correct records within 10 business days.
SecurityWritten security program (NIST CSF), encryption in transit and at rest, US hosting, role-based access, activity log.
BreachWritten notice to the school within 72 hours of confirming it, or sooner if state law requires.
SubprocessorsRender Services, Inc. (hosting, United States). 30 days' notice before adding another.
DeletionWithin 30 days of a written request; within 60 days after the contract ends, after an export if the school wants one. Certificate of deletion on request.
Minimum dataStudents as first name + last initial, or as codes in minimal-data mode. No student email, birthday, address or photos.
